# Top 3 Insights
# Revenue: 
No category dominates (Furniture leads at ₹3.52M, 21.6%; Electronics trails at ₹2.96M, 18.2%). Monthly sales swing between ₹2.47M and ₹2.96M because of order volume, with July the peak (220 orders) and June and October the dips. Sunday is the weakest weekday at 12.0% of sales versus 16.1% on Wednesday.

# Profitability:
Region is not where profit leaks (₹0.80M–₹0.94M, margins 26.7%–27.7%). Electronics is: 18.2% of sales but only 12.6% of profit, an 18.9% margin against 34.8% for Office Supplies. The gap shows in every product and region, and discounts are not the main cause.

# Customer behavior:
Corporate buys the most (26.5% of sales, average order ₹14,042). Cash is the top payment method (22.0%). UPI has the highest average order (₹14,429) but the lowest margin (26.1%) because of the heaviest discounts (13.8%).

# Strategic Recommendation
Launch an Electronics margin-recovery program next quarter (supplier cost renegotiation, repricing, 15% discount cap) to lift margin from 18.9% to about 24%, worth roughly +₹0.7L profit per quarter (estimate).
